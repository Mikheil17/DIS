RETURNS & REFUND POLICY

We want every adventurer to be happy with their purchase. Please take a moment to review our returns and refund information.

Last updated: June 26, 2026

RETURN ELIGIBILITY

If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may be eligible to request a return.

To be considered for a return, items should:

  • Be returned within 14 days of delivery.
  • Be unused and in their original condition.
  • Include their original packaging where applicable.

NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS

Certain items may not be eligible for returns, including personalized products, items damaged through misuse, or products that cannot be resold for hygiene reasons.

REFUNDS

Once a returned item has been received and inspected, we will notify you about the status of your refund. Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method.

DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ORDERS

Received something you didn't order, or did your package arrive damaged? Contact our support team with your order details so we can look into the issue.

ORDER CANCELLATIONS

If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible. We cannot guarantee cancellations once an order has entered processing or shipped.

CONTACT US

Have questions about a return or refund? Get in touch at returns@dungeondeals.com .
